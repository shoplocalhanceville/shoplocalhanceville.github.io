Sunny weekend ahead: trunk or treats, a barbecue cookoff and the last day of the county fair

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

Forecasters expect sunshine and highs in the low 70s through Sunday, which lines up nicely with a packed weekend of family fun across Cullman County.

If you have been waiting for a good weekend to get out, this is it. The National Weather Service expects sunny skies in Hanceville on Friday with a high near 75, then highs near 72 on Saturday and Sunday. Nights will be clear and chilly, dropping into the low 40s.

Friday evening, The Link Family Resource Center and its community partners host a free trunk or treat from 6 to 8 p.m. at the Desperation Church Student Center, 202 Elizabeth St. NE in Cullman. Expect decorated trunks, candy and food trucks, and costumes are welcome.

Saturday, The Butcher's Smoke and Flames BBQ Cookoff and Festival runs from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. at 266 Park Drive in Vinemont. Teams compete in a KCBS sanctioned contest, and there is also a People's Choice Boston butt contest, live music, a car show and vendors. Admission is free, according to event listings.

Saturday is also the final day of the Cullman County Fair at the fairgrounds on Sportsman Lake Road. Weekend gates open at 2 p.m., and the midway is cashless, so bring a card.

Closer to home, Wallace State's Preview Day runs from 9 a.m. to noon Saturday with hayride tours and pumpkin painting. Find these and more on our events page, each with directions and an add to calendar button.
