Hanceville City Council

Hanceville is run by a Mayor and five City Council members. Each one is elected to a four year term, and together they make the city’s laws, set its budget and decide where Hanceville goes next.

Council Members

Each council seat is called a Place. Here is who holds each seat, as listed by the City of Hanceville.

Place 1

Jana Shelton

Mayor Pro Tem

Elected August 2025. Term began November 2025.

Jana Shelton holds Place 1 on the City Council and serves as Mayor Pro Tem, so she steps in for the Mayor when he is absent. Voters elected her in August 2025, and the council picked her as Mayor Pro Tem in February 2026 after Nolan Bradford moved up to Mayor. At an April 2026 meeting she asked residents to volunteer with the Cullman County Commission on Aging and its Meals on Wheels program.

Contact: through City Hall at (256) 352-9830, 112 Main Street SE. The city does not publish a separate phone or email for this seat.

Place 2

Robert Powell

Councilor

Elected unopposed in 2025. Term began November 2025.

Robert Powell holds Place 2. He ran unopposed in 2025, and before joining the council he served on the city Planning Commission and taught government and economics at a Hanceville school. He has said he wants to modernize city records and put the ordinances online. In 2026 he brought forward proposals on zoning for payday lenders and pawn shops and on the use of city vehicles.

Contact: through City Hall at (256) 352-9830, 112 Main Street SE. The city does not publish a separate phone or email for this seat.

Place 3

Kim Brown

Councilor

Reelected August 2025 to her fifth term.

Kim Brown holds Place 3 and is serving her fifth term on the council. She was the only sitting member who ran again in 2025, and she said she wanted to help win back public trust after several city police officers were indicted. In February 2026 she cast the only vote against dissolving the city police department and municipal court.

Contact: through City Hall at (256) 352-9830, 112 Main Street SE. The city does not publish a separate phone or email for this seat.

Mayor

Mayor Nolan Bradford

Appointed Mayor Feb. 12, 2026.

Nolan Bradford has been Mayor since February 2026, when the council appointed him after Mayor Paul Campbell resigned. He was elected to the council in 2025 and was serving as Mayor Pro Tem at the time. Before that he spent about ten years in local roles, including chairing the Planning Commission and the Industrial Development Board and serving as vice chairman of the water board. In Hanceville the Mayor also votes as a member of the council.

Mayor’s Office: (256) 352-9811 · mayor@cityofhanceville.net · 112 Main Street SE

Meeting Schedule

The council meets on the second and fourth Thursday of every month at Hanceville City Hall. A work session starts at 6:00 PM, and the regular meeting follows right after. Every regular meeting is open to the public, and there is time for public comments.

What the Council Does

The council is the city’s lawmaking body. It works with the Mayor, city departments and residents to keep city services running and plan for the future. Its main jobs are to:

  • Pass city ordinances, resolutions and policies
  • Review and approve the city budget each year
  • Keep watch over how city money and property are used
  • Set up and support city services and departments
  • Plan road, drainage and other improvements, along with future growth
  • Appoint people to some city boards and offices, as state law allows
  • Speak up for the needs and concerns of Hanceville residents
