About Country Wok

Country Wok on Bangor Avenue serves Chinese American favorites plus newer plates like ramen, Japanese hot dogs, Hong Kong wontons and a matcha cannoli. Owner Kenny Lee keeps the menu focused and sources ingredients locally. Open Tuesday to Saturday 11 AM to 7 PM.
