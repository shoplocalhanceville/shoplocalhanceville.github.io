America's Thrift Stores plans a November opening in Cullman and is hiring now

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The secondhand store is moving into the former Bargain Hunt space at 1721 Second Ave. SW, and donations there support Make A Wish Alabama.

Bargain shoppers have a new stop coming. America's Thrift Stores plans to open a Cullman location in November at 1721 Second Ave. SW, Suite 1, The Cullman Tribune reported. That is the same address where Bargain Hunt operated before the chain closed stores in 2025. No exact opening day has been announced.

The store will sell secondhand clothing and household goods, sorted by category and size, with new items added to the floor every day. The company names Make A Wish Alabama as its charity partner in the state, so donations dropped off there help grant wishes for Alabama children with critical illnesses.

The company is hiring for full time and part time retail jobs and for processing jobs, which involve sorting, cleaning and pricing donated items. Anyone interested can apply through the careers page on the America's Thrift website.
