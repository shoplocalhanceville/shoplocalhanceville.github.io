About North Alabama Homebuilding Academy (NAHA)

The North Alabama Homebuilding Academy provides free hands on construction skills training through a 16 class bootcamp covering safety, hand tools, power saws, pneumatic nailers, and scaffolding. Students finish with focused training in a chosen trade such as electrical, plumbing, carpentry, or HVAC.
