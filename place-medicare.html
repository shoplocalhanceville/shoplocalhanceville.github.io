About Medicare

The official Medicare help line and website for questions about coverage, plans and claims.

Who it serves: People 65 and older and some younger people with disabilities.

medicare1-800-medicarehealth insuranceseniorsfederal

Hours

24 hours a day, 7 days a week, except some federal holidays.

Hours can change around holidays, so a quick call before you drive over is always a good idea.
