Wallace State volleyball brings a nine match win streak into Saturday

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The Lions swept Bishop State and Lurleen B. Wallace on Friday at Tom Drake Coliseum and finish their home invitational Saturday at 11 a.m. against Illinois Central College.

Wallace State volleyball is on a roll. The Lions are 25 and 5 overall and 11 and 2 in conference play after two straight set sweeps on the first day of the Wallace State Lions Invitational, according to the team's official schedule and results.

On Friday, Wallace State beat Bishop State 25 to 9, 25 to 16 and 25 to 11 in the noon match, then topped Lurleen B. Wallace 25 to 15, 25 to 16 and 25 to 17 that evening. That makes nine wins in a row.

The invitational wraps up Saturday, Oct. 17, with an 11 a.m. match against Illinois Central College at Tom Drake Coliseum on the Wallace State campus in Hanceville. Next week the Lions host Calhoun on Wednesday, Oct. 21, at 5 p.m. and Southern Union for a doubleheader on Thursday, Oct. 22. It is a great time to catch a match close to home.
