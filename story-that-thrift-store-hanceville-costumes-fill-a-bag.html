Hanceville's That Thrift Store has last minute Halloween costumes from 59 cents and a $5 fill a bag deal all year

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The family owned shop at 914 Main Street NE prices costumes from 59 cents to $5 and lets shoppers fill a bag with clothes for $5, with holiday decor priced low ahead of Thanksgiving and Christmas.

Still need a costume for tonight? That Thrift Store at 914 Main Street NE in Hanceville has a full rack of them, from witches and superheroes to a bright pink suit labeled "Gov. Ivey," The Cullman Tribune reported this week.

Costumes run from 59 cents to $5, and qualifying costumes can go into the store's $5 fill a bag clothing deal. Shoppers grab a Walmart size bag and fill it with any clothing they choose for $5.

Prefer to pick just a piece or two? Adult clothing is $1 each, and children's clothing in sizes 2T to 20 is 50 cents each. Baby clothing is also 50 cents and can go into a $5 bag when it is mixed with adult or children's clothes. Red sticker items are 25 cents and blue sticker items are half off, plus the store runs daily specials and surprise flash sales.

The shelves stay stocked after Halloween too, with freshly washed clothes, shoes, purses, vintage decor, glassware, kitchenware, books and collectibles, many priced from $2.99 to $24.99. The owners told the Tribune they price seasonal items low so families can decorate for Thanksgiving and Christmas without spending much.

That Thrift Store is open Wednesday through Saturday from 9 AM to 5 PM and closed Sunday through Tuesday. Today, Oct. 31, is a Saturday, so the doors are open until 5 PM. Call (256) 887-8037 or follow the store on Facebook for flash sales.
