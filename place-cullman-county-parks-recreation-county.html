About Cullman County Parks & Recreation (County)

Cullman County Parks and Recreation creates family oriented places of enjoyment for the people of Cullman County and its visitors. The department's parks include Sportsman Lake Park, Clarkson Covered Bridge, Stony Lonesome OHV Park, and the Cullman Agricultural Center, with events and entertainment throughout the year.
