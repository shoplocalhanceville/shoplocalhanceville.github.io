# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone involved with Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping to be treated with respect. We pledge to keep this project's spaces welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

* Being kind and welcoming to newcomers
* Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
* Publishing someone else's private information without permission
* Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
* Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a public, family-friendly space

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, and discussions on this repository — and in any public space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to shoplocalhanceville@gmail.com. Charles Adams, the project owner and only approver, reviews every report and decides the response, which can range from a private warning to a permanent ban from the project's spaces. Reporters' privacy is respected; details are not shared publicly.
