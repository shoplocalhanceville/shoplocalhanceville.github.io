Hanceville High on fall break through Monday, Red Ribbon Week starts Oct. 26

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Hanceville Today

Hanceville High students are out Friday, Oct. 16, and Monday, Oct. 19. Red Ribbon Week runs Oct. 26 to 30 with a different dress up theme each day.

Hanceville High students have a long weekend. Fall break covers Friday, Oct. 16, and Monday, Oct. 19, and it came right after a remote learning day on Thursday, Oct. 15, according to posts on the school's website.

When students get back, Red Ribbon Week is close behind, Oct. 26 to 30. The school's theme this year is “Make a Difference. Be a Hero. Stay Drug Free!” Each day has its own dress up theme as students and staff promise to make healthy choices.

Other Cullman County schools may be on different calendars, so check with your child's school for their dates.
