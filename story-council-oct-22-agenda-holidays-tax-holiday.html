Christmas plans, a storm prep tax holiday and hiring rules filled Hanceville's Oct. 22 council agenda

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The city posted the agenda for Thursday's council meeting. It lists the downtown trunk or treat, the Christmas parade and tree lighting, a severe weather sales tax holiday resolution and a plan to move the Thanksgiving Day meeting.

The City of Hanceville has posted the agenda for the council's Thursday, Oct. 22, meeting, and it gives a good look at what is coming up around town this fall and winter. The work session was set for 6 p.m. at City Hall, with the regular meeting right after.

Holiday plans take up a big part of the new business list. The council had the Downtown Trunk or Treat, the Tinsel Trail and Christmas Tree Lighting, and the Christmas Parade on the agenda. Dates for those events were not printed on the agenda, so we will add them to our events page as soon as the city or the organizers post them.

Also listed was Resolution 2026-1022 on the Severe Weather Preparedness Sales Tax Holiday. Alabama holds that holiday each February so families can stock up on things like flashlights, batteries and weather radios, and each city decides whether to drop its own sales tax for the weekend too.

Other new items included bill approval, reappointments to the Historic Preservation Commission, a tractor bid for Public Works and a plan to reschedule the Nov. 26 meeting, which falls on Thanksgiving Day.

Several items from the Oct. 8 meeting came back as old business. They include Councilman Robert Powell's take home vehicle policy, a citywide credit card policy, a formal interview process for city leadership jobs, and three ordinances on how the fire chief, public works director and park director are appointed.

Staff reports on bank balances, the fire department and Public Works and parks job details rounded out the night.

An agenda shows what the council planned to take up, not how members voted. We will share the results once the city posts minutes or video, or local reporters cover the meeting. The next regular meeting is Thursday, Nov. 12, at 6 p.m., and the new date for the second November meeting has not been announced yet.
