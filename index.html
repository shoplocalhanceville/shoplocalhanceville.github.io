Why Hanceville?

A small town with a big story, a college campus, peaceful parks and a downtown full of hometown charm.

Since 1879Hanceville was incorporated in May 1879, in an election held inside P. H. Kinney's general store. More fun facts
Wallace StateOne of the largest schools in the Alabama Community College System has called Hanceville home since 1966. Services
Four city parksBall fields, playgrounds, a community pool, walking trails and fishing at Veterans Memorial Park. Parks info
Historic downtownHome to a pharmacy and soda fountain serving the town since 1916, plus local eats and shops. Local eats
Visitors welcomeThe Shrine of the Most Blessed Sacrament draws visitors and pilgrims from around the world. Landmarks
Getting hereHanceville sits on U.S. Highway 31 in southern Cullman County, Alabama. City contacts
