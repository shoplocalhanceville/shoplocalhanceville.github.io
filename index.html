Listerhill Credit Union opens its new Cullman branch and gives $10,000 to Cullman County Schools

The credit union held a grand opening Thursday at 702 Industrial Dr. SW and handed Superintendent Dr. Shane Barnette a $10,000 check for the county school system.

Young performers can audition Oct. 20 for the For Kids' Sake Variety Show

The Cullman Regional Foundation is looking for singers, dancers, musicians and other young talent for its 2027 show, which raises money for nursery and pediatric care at Cullman Regional.

Around the County Oct 17, 2026

Curt's Closet is collecting toys for up to 600 Cullman County kids this Christmas

The local nonprofit's annual Curt's Christmas toy drive runs through mid November. You can drop off new toys at three spots in Cullman and Vinemont or sign up as a sponsor.

Around the County Oct 17, 2026

America's Thrift Stores plans a November opening in Cullman and is hiring now

The secondhand store is moving into the former Bargain Hunt space at 1721 Second Ave. SW, and donations there support Make A Wish Alabama.

City Hall Oct 17, 2026

Hanceville council adds $25,000 for road patching, backs mayor on fire chief

More gas tax money is going toward potholes, a take home vehicle policy is still up for debate, and the council upheld the mayor's removal of the fire chief. The next meeting is Thursday, Oct. 22, at 6 p.m.

Around the County Oct 17, 2026

Cullman marks MS Awareness Day with a free walk at Heritage Park today

Mayor Woody Jacobs proclaimed Oct. 17 Multiple Sclerosis Awareness Day. The Move Over MS Walk runs 8 a.m. to noon at Heritage Park with music, free snacks and a kids costume contest.
