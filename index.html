Site information & policies

About this site

Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping is a local news organization for Hanceville and all of Cullman County, Alabama. It publishes dated local news from recorded sources and provides the Community Guide, a free directory that helps neighbors find local businesses, restaurants, churches, schools, nonprofits, city services, and help resources, with real phone numbers, directions, and the latest posts from local Facebook pages. The site is run by Charles Adams of Hanceville, Alabama. Listings are free, and nothing is published without the owner’s approval.

Newsroom practice and corrections

News items on this site are prepared from recorded sources and are dated so readers can judge the age of the information. The site separates news from opinion and labels sponsored material when payment or another material connection exists. When the site learns that a published news item contains a material error, the site will correct the item and will mark the correction in the item with the date of the correction. Corrections are also recorded on the News page, with their dates. A reader who believes that a news item is wrong may send the specific claim and a reliable source by message to the Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping Facebook Page so the site can check the record.

Read the corrections record on the News page

Privacy policy

This site does not require accounts, does not ask for passwords, and does not sell or share visitor information. The site itself sets no tracking cookies. Places you save are stored only in your own browser on your own device and never leave it. Search runs on your device. Weather comes from the National Weather Service. When you tap “See latest post” on a listing, that organization’s Facebook post loads from Facebook, and Facebook’s own privacy policy then applies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like most hosts, GitHub may keep standard server logs. Questions about privacy can be sent by message to the Hanceville Entertainment & Shopping Facebook Page.
