Shrimp Basket closes its Cullman restaurant as the chain refocuses on the coast

Published Nov 6, 2026 by Hanceville Today

Artistry Restaurants shuttered the Cullman Shrimp Basket effective Nov. 1, part of a wider pullback from inland locations so the brand can put its energy into beach and coastal stores.

Shrimp Basket's Cullman restaurant is closed. Parent company Artistry Restaurants said the Cullman location shut effective Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, along with other inland stores in Alabama and one in the Pensacola area.

CEO Bryan Lockwood said the company is returning the brand to its coastal seafood roots and directing future investment toward beach and coastal restaurants. Alabama closures also include Bay Minette, Gardendale, Tuscaloosa and Auburn, leaving Foley, Gulf Shores, Orange Beach and Evergreen among the Alabama stores still open.

Locals who counted on the Cullman stop for smoked mahi dip, Boom Boom shrimp and other favorites will need to plan a coastal trip or find another hometown seafood option. Shop Local Alabama will keep pointing neighbors to Cullman County kitchens that are still plating fresh meals close to home.
