About Cameron Rowe

Cameron Rowe holds Place 4. He won the seat in August 2025 and said his goals were to rebuild trust in the council and make the town more inviting. On Oct. 8, 2026, the council appointed him to the Planning Commission.

Term: Elected August 2025. Term began November 2025.
