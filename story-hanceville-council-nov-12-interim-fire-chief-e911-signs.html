Hanceville names an interim fire chief, orders purple and white E-911 road signs and moves its Christmas Eve meeting

Published Nov 16, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The Hanceville City Council on Nov. 12 named Bart Absher interim fire chief, set a Dec. 10 public hearing on a rezoning request and moved its Dec. 24 meeting to Dec. 29. The next work session and meeting is Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 6 PM.

Hanceville has a new leader at the fire department, at least for now. At its Thursday, Nov. 12, meeting, the City Council named Bart Absher interim fire chief. The post had been open since the council upheld the removal of the previous chief on Oct. 8.

The council also appointed James Helms to the Hanceville Housing Authority board, replacing Edna Swann.

Drivers will soon notice new E-911 road signs. The signs have been ordered in a purple and white color scheme and will run along Perth Street to U.S. Highway 31.

A public hearing is set for Thursday, Dec. 10, on a rezoning request filed by Eight27 Holdings and Del Bales. Residents can speak on the proposed zoning change before the council takes any action.

On the money side, the council gave the Community Development Committee $1,000 through a budget amendment and approved the committee's Christmas tree entry in the Civitan Tinsel Trail, with the city paying the $25 registration fee. The city's donation to the Civitan club doubled from $500 to $1,000.

Members approved an agreement that lets the city's GIS mapping connect with its BS&A software, with a one time payment of $2,500 and $250 a month after that, about $5,500 in the first year. They also approved a $14,253 contract with AirMed.

Because Dec. 24 is Christmas Eve, the council moved that meeting to Tuesday, Dec. 29. The next regular work session and meeting is Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 6 PM at Hanceville City Hall, 112 Main St SE. Meetings are open to the public.
