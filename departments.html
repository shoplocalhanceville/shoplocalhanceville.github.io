Hanceville Fire and Rescue

Fire station, non emergency lineFire chief post vacant after Oct. 8, 2026

Phone
(256) 352-5544
Emergency
911
Address
119 Collins Avenue, Hanceville, AL 35077

The fire department answers calls around the clock. Use the non emergency line for burn permits, the free smoke alarm program and general questions, and call 911 in an emergency. The council upheld the removal of the fire chief on Oct. 8, 2026, and no replacement had been named when we checked.

Phone

Emergency

Address

The city dissolved its police department and municipal court in February 2026, and Cullman County deputies have been patrolling Hanceville since. The council is working on policies to bring back a city police department. Call 911 for any emergency.
