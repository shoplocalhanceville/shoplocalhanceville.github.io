Hanceville weekend weather: sunny days, cold nights

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The National Weather Service expects sunshine Saturday through Monday in Hanceville, with highs in the 60s and lows near 40. Saturday will be windy.

You'll want a jacket in the morning and sunglasses by lunch. The National Weather Service office in Huntsville expects a sunny Saturday in Hanceville with a high near 63. A north wind around 15 mph could gust as high as 30 mph.

Saturday night looks clear and cold, with a low around 40. Sunday stays sunny with a high near 67 before dropping to about 38 overnight. Monday brings more sun and a high near 69.

It's a nice weekend for a pumpkin patch, a ballgame or a walk downtown. This forecast came out early Saturday, Oct. 17, and forecasts change, so check the Weather Service before you head out.
