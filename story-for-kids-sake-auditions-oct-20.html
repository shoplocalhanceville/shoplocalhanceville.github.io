Young performers can audition Oct. 20 for the For Kids' Sake Variety Show

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The Cullman Regional Foundation is looking for singers, dancers, musicians and other young talent for its 2027 show, which raises money for nursery and pediatric care at Cullman Regional.

Got a kid who sings in the car, dances in the kitchen or plays an instrument? The Cullman Regional Foundation is holding auditions next week for the 2027 Bownes Memorial For Kids' Sake Variety Show, The Cullman Tribune reported.

Auditions are Tuesday, Oct. 20, from 4 to 7:30 p.m. in Christ Hall at St. John's Church in Cullman. Thursday, Oct. 22, auditions are by appointment. The application form is linked in the Tribune's story.

The show goes back to 1993, when the late Dr. Eugene Bownes, a Cullman pediatrician, noticed how much musical and theatrical talent his young patients had. Since then it has raised more than $100,000 for the Nursery and Pediatrics Fund at Cullman Regional, and last year's show had more than 60 young performers. Performers help raise money by making a donation at their audition and by selling tickets and sponsorships.

The date and venue for the 2027 show will be announced later. For questions, contact the Cullman Regional Foundation at 256-737-2565 or crmcfoundation@cullmanregional.com.
