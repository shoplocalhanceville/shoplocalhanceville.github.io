About Cullman County DHR Office

Your local Department of Human Resources office for food assistance, family assistance, child support, adult services and family and child services. One main number reaches every department.

Who it serves: Cullman County residents.

dhrhuman resourcesfood stampstanfchild support

Hours

8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Hours can change around holidays, so a quick call before you drive over is always a good idea.
