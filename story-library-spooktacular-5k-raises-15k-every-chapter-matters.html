Library's Spooktacular 5K draws more than 300 runners, raises over $15,000 and kicks off an expansion campaign

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The Cullman County Public Library is launching Every Chapter Matters, a campaign for a renovated and expanded library.

More than 300 runners and walkers turned out Saturday, Oct. 24, for the Spooktacular Storybook 5K and 1 Mile Fun Run, a fundraiser for the Cullman County Public Library, The Cullman Tribune reported. The race raised more than $15,000, though a final total after expenses was not yet available.

Assistant Director Shelby Creekmore said the costumes and the support from families were highlights, and that the library already has more plans for next year's run.

The race also kicks off the library's capital campaign, called Every Chapter Matters. The campaign will teach the community about library services and raise money to renovate and expand the library building.

Director Amber Thornton said the library logged more than 100,000 visits in 2026 and saved patrons about $1.5 million. She said the goal is a library with more space, more programs and better access to resources. You can learn more and find ways to help at cullmanlib.org, and race results are posted on RunningLane.
