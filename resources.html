More Resources: State and Federal Help

These programs are run by the State of Alabama, the federal government and Cullman County agencies rather than the City of Hanceville. For city departments, see Departments and Contacts.

Need help right now?

If you or someone else is in danger, call 911. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988. For domestic violence, call 1‑800‑650‑6522. All are free and open 24 hours.
