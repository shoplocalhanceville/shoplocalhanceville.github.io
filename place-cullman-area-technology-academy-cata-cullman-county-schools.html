About Cullman Area Technology Academy (CATA), Cullman County Schools

The Cullman Area Technology Academy is Cullman County Schools' career and technical education academy on Highway 31 North in Cullman, Alabama. Students attend CATA from their home schools for career programs and hands on instruction that can lead to industry recognized credentials for college and the workforce.
