# Writing Style Guide

The site should read like a neighbor who knows Hanceville wrote it.

## Rules checked by `tools/audit.py`
* No em dashes or en dashes. Use a comma, a period, parentheses, or the word "to" for ranges (8 AM to 5 PM, K-12 with a hyphen is fine).
* No double spaces.
* Spelling passes codespell (names and Spanish words go in `tools/codespell-ignore.txt`).

## Rules for people and bots writing copy
* Complete sentences. Every listing blurb has a subject and a verb.
* Facts only from the business's own site or Facebook Page, government sources, or two reliable sources that agree. Never guess hours, prices or owners.
* Say where and who it serves: street, town, who it is for.
* Vary sentence openings. Do not start three listings in a row the same way.
* Avoid filler and cliches: "nestled", "hidden gem", "look no further", "whether you're", "elevate", "seamless", "vibrant", "in today's world", "a testament to".
* No internal notes on public pages (no "verify", "TODO", "candidate", research labels or owner names).
* Plain words over big ones. "Help" beats "facilitate".
* Times: 8 AM, 5:30 PM. Dates: Oct. 24. Phone: (256) 555-0100.
* Hashtags and emoji belong on social posts, not on site copy.
