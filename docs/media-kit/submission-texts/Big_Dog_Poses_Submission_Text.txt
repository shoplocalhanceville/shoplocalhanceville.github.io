WHO BIG DOG IS
Big Dog is the bulldog mascot of Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping. He is a friendly brindle and white bulldog with a purple tint in his coat, and he is the face of the Big Dog Approved seal. When Big Dog appears in a video, he represents the Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping page and its promise that local business information is checked, honest, and safe.

WHAT THIS FILE CONTAINS
The file holds 18 pictures of Big Dog. Page 1 is an index that shows every pose with its number. Each pose then appears on its own full page after the index. Every picture is vertical 9:16, and each one shows Big Dog alone on a plain white background so he can be cut out cleanly and placed into a video.

17. Standing with both paws spread wide in welcome
18. Holding a blank white sign on a stick

HOW TO USE THE POSES
When I ask for a video, use the pose number I name. Cut Big Dog out of the white background and keep him exactly as he is drawn. Keep his colors, his markings, and his friendly look the same in every video. Make the finished video vertical 9:16 unless I ask for a different size. Pose 7 is for pointing at words or a business name placed beside him. Pose 12 is for anything that is verified or approved. Pose 18 is blank on purpose, so put the words I give you on that sign and no other words.

RULES
Never change Big Dog into a different dog, and never change his colors. Do not add words, logos, or other characters unless I ask for them in that video. Keep every video family friendly. The page colors are black, purple, and white, so use those colors for backgrounds and words when I ask for a designed scene.

MEET MAGGIE
The last section of the file introduces Maggie, Big Dog's girlfriend. Maggie is a brindle and white bulldog with a purple tint, and she wears a big purple bow with gold sparkles and a purple collar with a gold heart tag. Her section has two full pages. The first page is Maggie by herself, and the second page is Big Dog and Maggie together. Keep her bow and her collar on her in every video, and keep her look exactly as she is drawn. Maggie may appear by herself or together with Big Dog when I ask for her.
