WHAT TO BUILD FIRST
Follow Section 3 of the implementation file, in order: the homepage welcome with the together welcoming pose DUO-F17, the section accents with their named poses, the Business Spotlight art with the checkmark pose and the Big Dog Approved seal, the profile page accents, and the notice art. Build website copies as WebP files under img/mascots/ from the PNG masters, keep the masters untouched, lazy load the images, and keep the total added homepage weight under 400 kilobytes.

THE RULES THAT MATTER MOST
The characters are locked: never redraw or recolor Big Dog or Maggie, and Maggie's purple bow and her purple collar with the gold heart tag never come off. Use poses by their catalog IDs. Change no listing data under this build. Keep the site email off the site. Run the security and accuracy scans before and after, and report exactly what you built.

And the standing rule above all: build it, show me the result, and nothing goes live until I approve it.
