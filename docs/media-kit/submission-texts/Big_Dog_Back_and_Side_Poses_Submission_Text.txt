WHO BIG DOG IS
Big Dog is the bulldog mascot of Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping. He is a friendly brindle and white bulldog with a purple tint in his coat, and he is the face of the Big Dog Approved seal.

WHAT THIS FILE CONTAINS
The file holds 8 pictures of Big Dog seen from behind and from the side. Page 1 is an index that shows every pose with its number. Each pose then appears on its own full page after the index. Every picture is vertical 9:16, and each one shows Big Dog alone on a plain white background so he can be cut out cleanly and placed into a video.

THE 8 POSES
1. Back view, standing and waving with one paw raised
2. Back view, sitting
3. Back view, walking away
4. Back view, standing with both paws up and his tail wagging
5. Side profile facing right, standing
6. Side profile facing right, sitting
7. Side profile facing right, running
8. Side profile facing right, pointing forward

HOW TO USE THE POSES
When I ask for a video, use the pose number I name. Cut Big Dog out of the white background and keep him exactly as he is drawn. Keep his colors, his markings, and his look the same in every video. Make the finished video vertical 9:16 unless I ask for a different size. The back view poses are for shots where Big Dog walks away, waves goodbye, or is seen from behind. The side profile poses are for shots where he walks, runs, or points across the screen.

RULES
Never change Big Dog into a different dog, and never change his colors. Do not add words, logos, or other characters unless I ask for them in that video. Keep every video family friendly. The page colors are black, purple, and white, so use those colors for backgrounds and words when I ask for a designed scene.

EXAMPLES OF WHAT I MAY ASK
"Use pose 3 and make Big Dog walk away at the end of the video while he waves goodbye."
"Use pose 7 and make Big Dog run across the screen."
"Use pose 8 and make Big Dog point at the business name beside him."
