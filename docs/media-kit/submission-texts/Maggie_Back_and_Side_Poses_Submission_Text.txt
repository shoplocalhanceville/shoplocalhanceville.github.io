WHO MAGGIE IS
Maggie is the girlfriend of Big Dog, the bulldog mascot of Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping. She is a friendly brindle and white bulldog with a purple tint in her coat. She wears a big purple bow with gold sparkles on one ear and a purple collar with a gold heart tag, and her bow and collar stay on her in every picture and every video.

WHAT THIS FILE CONTAINS
The file holds 8 pictures of Maggie seen from behind and from the side. Page 1 is an index that shows every pose with its number. Each pose then appears on its own full page after the index. Every picture is vertical 9:16, and each one shows Maggie alone on a plain white background so she can be cut out cleanly and placed into a video.

HOW TO USE THE POSES
When I ask for a video, use the pose number I name. Cut Maggie out of the white background and keep her exactly as she is drawn. Keep her colors, her markings, her bow, her collar, and her look the same in every video. Make the finished video vertical 9:16 unless I ask for a different size. The back view poses are for shots where Maggie walks away, waves goodbye, or is seen from behind. The side profile poses are for shots where she walks, runs, or points across the screen. Maggie may appear by herself or together with Big Dog when I ask for both of them.

RULES
Never change Maggie into a different dog, and never change her colors or remove her bow and collar. Do not add words, logos, or other characters unless I ask for them in that video. Keep every video family friendly. The page colors are black, purple, and white, so use those colors for backgrounds and words when I ask for a designed scene.
