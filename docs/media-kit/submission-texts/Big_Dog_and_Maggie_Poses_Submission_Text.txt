WHO THEY ARE
Big Dog is the bulldog mascot of Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping, a friendly brindle and white bulldog with a purple tint in his coat. Maggie is his girlfriend. She is a brindle and white bulldog with a purple tint, and she wears a big purple bow with gold sparkles and a purple collar with a gold heart tag. Together they represent the Hanceville Entertainment and Shopping page and its promise that local business information is checked, honest, and safe.

HOW TO USE THE POSES
When I ask for a video, use the pose number I name. Cut Big Dog and Maggie out of the white background and keep them exactly as they are drawn. Keep their colors, their markings, and their friendly look the same in every video. Keep Maggie's purple bow and her collar with the gold heart on her at all times. Make the finished video vertical 9:16 unless I ask for a different size. Pose 7 is for pointing at words or a business name placed beside them. Pose 12 is for anything that is verified or approved. Pose 18 is blank on purpose, so put the words I give you on that sign and no other words.

RULES
Never change Big Dog or Maggie into different dogs, and never change their colors. Do not add words, logos, or other characters unless I ask for them in that video. Keep every video family friendly. The page colors are black, purple, and white, so use those colors for backgrounds and words when I ask for a designed scene.
