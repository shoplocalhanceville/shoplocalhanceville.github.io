HOW TO USE THE POSES
When I ask for a video, use the pose number I name. Cut Big Dog and Maggie out of the white background and keep them exactly as they are drawn. Keep their colors, their markings, and their look the same in every video, and keep Maggie's bow and collar on her at all times. Make the finished video vertical 9:16 unless I ask for a different size. The back view poses are for shots where the two of them walk away, wave goodbye, or are seen from behind. The side profile poses are for shots where they walk, run, or point across the screen together.

RULES
Never change Big Dog or Maggie into different dogs, and never change their colors. Do not add words, logos, or other characters unless I ask for them in that video. Keep every video family friendly. The page colors are black, purple, and white, so use those colors for backgrounds and words when I ask for a designed scene.
