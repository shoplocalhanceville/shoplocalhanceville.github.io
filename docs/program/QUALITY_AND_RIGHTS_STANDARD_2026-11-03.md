# Quality and Rights Standard — directed by Charles Adams, 2026-11-03

Applies to every lane, every workstream, and every aspect of the website and its content, from the beginning of the site forward. It sits under the Charles Development Standard and is folded into the canonical standard text at its next version.

## 1. Accuracy before building — no guessing, no assuming, no shortcuts

Nothing is guessed, assumed, or done lazily. Before anything is built, developed, coded, structured, organized, labeled, or written, the facts it rests on are verified and provable. This covers every surface of the work, not only listing data: code, structure, organization, labels, writing, spelling, letters, fonts, pictures, images, and video, at standard and advanced levels alike. Where a fact cannot be verified, the honest gap is recorded and the work waits or proceeds without it; a gap is never filled with a plausible invention. Spelling and grammar in anything published or filed are checked, not assumed.

## 2. Documentation as the work happens

Every build carries its records with it: detailed notes, the final state of what was decided and done, coding sheets for how a thing is built, cheat sheets for how it is operated or repeated, and troubleshooting entries written when an issue is met, in the form the program masters already use (master guide, emergency playbook, troubleshooting guide and cases, error codes). An issue encountered and not written down the same pass is treated as not finished. These records are part of the deliverable, not an afterthought.

## 3. Copyright and rights compliance

Everything is developed to comply with copyright and the law, so that Charles does not break the law and no one else's rights are infringed. This covers the entire website and everything connected to it, including raw-coded work, posts, articles, write-ups, pictures, images, and video.

- Original work first: content, code, and media are created for this site or drawn from sources with a clear right to use them.
- Real logos, photos, and portraits follow the existing provenance rules: the organization's own real logo or picture, recorded in the logo and image provenance ledger with its source; where no right or no real image is verified, an honestly labeled site-made graphic is used instead.
- No third-party text, article, image, video, music, or code is copied in without a license, permission, or a clear legal basis; attribution is given wherever a license or the source requires it.
- If the rights in any item cannot be verified, the item is not used. Uncertainty resolves against use, every time.
- The validation lane runs a rights check on media and copied material before any package is presented for publishing, the same way it checks facts and rendering.

## 4. General legal compliance

Beyond copyright: paid placements are labeled as sponsored (see Workstream 2), no claim is published that the records cannot support (no promised results, no invented numbers), privacy practice follows the program's privacy page commitment, and where a rule of law is uncertain the item is flagged in the decision list for Charles rather than resolved by assumption.
