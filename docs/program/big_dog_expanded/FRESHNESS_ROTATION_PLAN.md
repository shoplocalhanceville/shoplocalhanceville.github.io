# Freshness Rotation Plan — Expanded Big Dog

Purpose: the website always has something new prepared ahead of time, so growth never depends on a same day scramble and never stalls when Charles is unreachable. Every rotation item is prepared, sourced, and validated in advance, and publishes under the authorizations in force in the program.

## Always prepared ahead

1. Business Spotlight rotation: a ranked queue of at least four spotlights prepared at all times, each with a verified description, a rights cleared logo or photo recorded in the provenance ledger, and its Spanish translation. When one spotlight runs, the next one enters preparation the same week.
2. This Week in Hanceville: the weekly post and site strip prepared from the standing Monday content engine, using the approved Canva templates in black, purple, and white.
3. New in Town badge queue: new listings verified and held ready with the badge applied in staging, so a new business can be featured the day its listing is approved.
4. Seasonal calendar: twelve months mapped in advance for Hanceville and Cullman County, covering school terms, local events, holidays, and sports seasons, including Cullman County Gridiron promotion windows. Each month gets one prepared theme brief two months ahead.
5. School information strip: closings and lunch menus prepared from official sources only, refreshed on the school schedule, never carried past their date.
6. Design refresh cycle: one small, gate safe design improvement briefed every month from the Design Integration cell, drawn from the prevention register aging review. A refresh that risks a Lighthouse score is redesigned or dropped, never shipped on hope.
7. Media refresh cycle: one prepared image or video improvement briefed every month from the Media Pipeline cell, sized per use without losing integrity, with provenance recorded before staging.

## Rules

Nothing rotates in with an unverified fact, an uncleared image, or a missing Spanish version where the surface carries Spanish. A prepared item that passes its date unused is reviewed once, then retired or renewed with fresh sourcing. The queue depth is reported in every pass report: spotlights ready, weeks prepared, and briefs staged, so a thin queue is visible before it becomes a stall.
