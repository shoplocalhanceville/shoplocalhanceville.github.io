# Prevention and Freshness Register — Expanded Big Dog

Owner: Prevention and Freshness cell. Purpose: find problems before they reach a visitor, and keep the website changing and growing so it never goes stale. Every finding becomes one brief on the standard brief template. Nothing in this register publishes by itself.

## Standing checks

1. Link health: every listing link and profile link checked on the standing six hour security and accuracy watch. A link that fails twice in a row, or lands on a parked, hijacked, or spam injected page, is briefed for removal or a verified replacement the same day it is confirmed. Known bad domains stay on the never link list permanently.
2. Injected content watch: linked outside pages that have carried injected spam before, including the state senior services pages, are rechecked on every watch cycle. A recurrence is briefed immediately.
3. Hours and season expiry: listings with verified hours are rechecked when a season changes and whenever a source page changes. Events carry an end date in the data, and an expired event is briefed for removal or renewal the day after it ends.
4. Listing fact aging: any listing whose recorded source is older than ninety days enters the freshness queue for reverification, oldest first. A fact that cannot be reverified is marked honestly in the record, never silently kept as current.
5. Media aging: logos and photos are reviewed when a business changes its public branding, and every media item is spot checked on the quarterly pass. Replacement media enters only with its provenance recorded.
6. Design aging: any homepage rail, tab, or profile element that has not changed in sixty days is reviewed by Design Integration for a refresh brief, subject to the theme and the Lighthouse budget.
7. Standards watch: Lighthouse version changes, Core Web Vitals changes, WCAG developments, and GitHub Pages platform changes are tracked by Research and Development. A change that could move a score is briefed before the next release, not after a score drops.
8. Score trend: the per release score ledger is reviewed after every release. Two releases in a row moving the same metric down opens a prevention brief on that metric.
9. Error hunting: raw code errors across the whole site are hunted on every staged package that touches the affected files, and a full site error pass runs monthly. Findings follow the Emergency Playbook rule: reproduce by rendering first.
10. Continuity: this register and the brief queue are the work itself. They run on schedule whether or not Charles is reachable, so building, staging, and validation never wait on his phone. Publishing follows the authorizations in force in the program.
11. Visual detection: every staged package, every theme or layout change, and a full site sweep monthly are rendered and screenshotted at phone, tablet, and desktop widths and inspected under the Detail Prevention and Visual Detection Protocol. Imperfections are entered here with their screenshots and become raw code fix briefs. Upgrade candidates are logged separately for Charles's decision list.

## Cadence

Six hour watch for items 1 and 2. Weekly pass for items 3, 5, and 6. Per release for item 8. Monthly for item 9 full pass and item 4 queue review. Quarterly for the item 5 full media review.
