# Team Charter — restructured for the Complete Overhaul Program, 2026-11-03

Set by Charles Adams's direction on 2026-11-03: restructure the team for highly professional development, able to expand and build out the website continuously without asking continuously, toward the 100 out of 100 Lighthouse standard and professional standards generally, with accounting, organizing, and forward-looking score work as named duties.

## The lanes

**Lane 1 — Build (Big Dog).** Owns all coding, development, and implementation in raw code: staged packages for the overhaul workstreams, the publish queue, fixes from the register. Works continuously from the confirmed plans; does not stop mid-job for questions it can resolve from the masters, the register, or the standards.

**Lane 2 — Validation (independent checker).** No builder validates its own work (Development Standard, seven-step chain). Owns render-and-exercise verification of every staged package and every live release, gate runs and score capture, the manual accessibility layer, and the six-hour security and accuracy watch. Reports measured results only. This lane also owns the verification standard Charles directed on 2026-11-03: everything on the site valid and confirmed, no misinformation, and a backup reference recorded for the information — every listing and every news item carries its source in the sources registry, and a fact with no source is researched or held, never published on assumption.

**Lane 3 — Research and forward looking (Maggie).** Owns planning briefs and specs for Lane 1, the standards watch (Lighthouse version changes, Core Web Vitals changes, WCAG and platform developments), the score ledger and trend line (every release's measured scores, with the Lighthouse version pinned beside them, per gap item 1), and preparation of the next packages so the build lane never waits. Social, creative, and content drafting (the weekly content engine) also sits in this lane. News drafting follows the news-first positioning Charles set on 2026-11-03: the site presents as a local news organization with the Community Guide as a featured service within it, and every news item is drafted sourced and dated, with corrections recorded when facts change.

**Lane 4 — Accounting and organizing (Big Dog, records duty).** Owns the program masters as the single source of truth: listings master, files registry, features registry, releases log, decisions and holds, workstreams, roles, the imperfections register, the sponsor ledger, and this charter. Every release, decision, and score lands in the records in the same pass as the work itself. Nothing is "done" until its record is written.

**Grok** — freshness passes on the site (6:45 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. CT) and build support under its instruction file. **Copilot** — coding assistance only. Both receive this restructure when Charles hands them the updated instruction files; until then their current instructions stand.

## Quality and rights (all lanes, directed by Charles 2026-11-03)

Every lane works under QUALITY_AND_RIGHTS_STANDARD_2026-11-03.md: nothing guessed or assumed on any surface (code, structure, labels, writing, spelling, fonts, pictures, images, video); documentation written as the work happens, including detailed troubleshooting notes when an issue is met; and full copyright and legal compliance, with the validation lane running a rights check on media and copied material before any package is presented, and any item whose rights cannot be verified not used at all.

## The autonomy model (how the team builds without asking continuously)

1. Lanes work continuously inside the confirmed program and the confirmed plans. Mid-job questions are resolved from the masters, the register, and the Development Standard, not escalated.
2. Anything that truly requires Charles personally is batched into ONE decision list at the end of a pass (his standing instruction of 2026-11-03), never dripped out one question at a time.
3. Staged packages accumulate ready, each with its verification evidence, and are presented in batches for approval.
4. The one boundary that does not move: nothing publishes without Charles's approval of the named package. He is the only approver. This charter widens everything up to that line — preparation, building, staging, verification, records — and the line itself stays, because it is his protection against exactly the failures this program exists to prevent (a release verified by bytes alone once broke the homepage).
5. Emergencies follow the Emergency Playbook: reproduce by rendering first, fix in staging, verify, then present.

## Score duty (forward-looking)

The score ledger records, per release: the four Lighthouse category medians per tested page, the Lighthouse version, the gate outcome, and the date. Lane 3 reports the trend to Charles with each pass report: where the score stands, what moved it, and what the next package is expected to do — expected, never promised.
