Last call: Parkside Outreach Program's Christmas Coalition takes applications through Sunday, Nov. 1

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The Baileyton nonprofit helps local families in need with Christmas for their children. Applications are due Nov. 1, and sponsors can adopt an angel for $25.

Families who could use help with Christmas this year have until Sunday, Nov. 1, to apply for the Parkside Outreach Program's Christmas Coalition, the Baileyton nonprofit's holiday help program now in its ninth year.

Applications are on the program's website at parksideoutreach.com, on its Facebook Page, or in person at the Parkside Outreach store, 12756 Alabama Highway 69 in Baileyton. The program asks for one application per household, and applicants must live within a 10 mile radius of Parkside Outreach Program.

Bring proof of residence for every child listed and proof of all household income. A SNAP allotment letter counts. Every application and document must be in by the deadline. Approved families will hear back by Nov. 13, and pickups are the week of Dec. 14.

Want to help instead? Parkside is looking for community and church sponsors. Its website lets donors adopt an angel for $25, and sponsors can call (256) 506-5545 or message the program on Facebook. Money spent at the Parkside thrift store also goes back to the community through its food pantry.

Questions about applying can go to (256) 683-0715.
