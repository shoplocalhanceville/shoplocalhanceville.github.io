About Hanceville Entertainment & Shopping

Who we are

Hanceville Entertainment & Shopping, also known as Shop Local Alabama, is an independent community page. We share the people, places and small businesses that make Hanceville and Cullman County feel like home.

We are not the Chamber of Commerce and we are not part of the City of Hanceville. City details on this site link back to the city's own pages so you can always check the original.

What you will find here

The directory lists 721 businesses, offices, nonprofits and services, and every one has its own page. You can browse by name from A to Z, by category, or start with City Services, the Business Directory, Local News and Upcoming Events.

How we check listings

We only publish details we can confirm on an official source, such as a business's own website, its own Facebook Page, a listing the owner has claimed, or a public record. When we cannot confirm something, we leave it off rather than guess. Every listing shows its sources on our Sources and References page.

Listings are free

Business owners can ask to be added, corrected or removed at any time. See our Contact page or message our Facebook Page.
