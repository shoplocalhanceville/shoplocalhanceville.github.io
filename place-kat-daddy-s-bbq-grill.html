About Kat Daddy's BBQ & Grill

Kat Daddy's BBQ & Grill is a family owned barbecue restaurant in Bremen, Alabama. The menu centers on smoked meats such as pulled pork and ribs, along with blackened catfish, burgers, and homemade pies. The owners describe it in their own profile as a warm spot where they love their customers and enjoy meeting new faces.
