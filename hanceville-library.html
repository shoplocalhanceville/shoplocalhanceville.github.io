Shirley Burden Public Library, Hanceville

FreeLibraryCards Now

Library cards from the Cullman County Public Library System are free for people who live, work or go to school in Cullman County. One card works at all five county libraries, including ours right here in Hanceville, plus the digital catalogs.

What to bring: a filled out card application, a valid photo ID, and proof of address if the address on your ID is not current. Kids under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign for the card.

Call (256) 352-0685Get the card application

This WeekAt TheLibrary

  • Wednesday, 10 AM: Children's Story Time with a craft and a snack.
  • Every open day: free computers and free Wi-Fi.

The library posts new events each week on its Facebook Page, Hanceville Public Library. Check there before you go.

StartReadingToday

Books, audiobooks, DVDs and digital resources for every age. Bring the kids to Story Time, sit a while in the outdoor children's garden, or grab a used book at one of the library's book sales.

Plan your visit

Email: hancevillepubliclibrary@gmail.com

Hours: Tuesday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM (closed noon to 1 PM for lunch). Saturday, 9 AM to 1 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday.
