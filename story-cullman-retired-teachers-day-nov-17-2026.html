Cullman sets Nov. 17 as Retired Teachers Day, so thank a teacher

Published Nov 8, 2026 by Hanceville Today

Mayor Woody Jacobs signed a proclamation naming Tuesday, Nov. 17, Retired Teachers Day in the City of Cullman. Members of the Cullman Education Retirees Association accepted it on Nov. 5.

If a teacher made a difference in your life, Tuesday, Nov. 17, is a good day to tell them. Cullman Mayor Woody Jacobs has proclaimed it Retired Teachers Day in the City of Cullman.

Four retired educators accepted the proclamation on Thursday, Nov. 5, on behalf of the Cullman Education Retirees Association, a local affiliate of the Alabama Education Retirees Association. The proclamation recognizes the role public education has played in the growth and character of Cullman and the teachers who shaped generations of local students.

“We appreciate everything our retired teachers have done for our children and our community,” Jacobs said. “Thank you for your years of dedication and service.”

Alabama marks Retired Teachers Day on the Tuesday of American Education Week each year. The city encourages everyone to take a moment and thank a retired teacher.
