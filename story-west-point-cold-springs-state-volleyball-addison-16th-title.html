West Point volleyball reaches the state semifinals as Addison wins its 16th state title

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

West Point and Cold Springs closed their seasons at the state tournament in Birmingham, and neighboring Addison beat Meek for a second straight Class 1A championship.

Two Cullman County volleyball teams wrapped up their seasons this week at the AHSAA state tournament at Bill Harris Arena in Birmingham, and a nearby rival brought home another state championship.

West Point opened Class 4A play on Wednesday, Oct. 28, with a 3 to 0 sweep of Satsuma. The Warriors met defending champion Guntersville in the semifinals that afternoon and fell 25 to 13, 25 to 17 and 25 to 22. Guntersville went on to beat Orange Beach on Thursday for its second straight Class 4A title.

Cold Springs earned its trip to Birmingham with a five set win over Pisgah at the North Super Regional in Hoover. The Eagles drew No. 5 Ariton in the Class 2A quarterfinals on Oct. 28 and lost 3 to 0.

In Class 1A, Addison beat Winston County rival Meek 3 to 1 on Oct. 28, winning 25 to 11, 21 to 25, 25 to 23 and 26 to 24. It is the Bulldogs' second straight state championship and the 16th in program history. Ava Bartlett was named tournament MVP with 21 kills, three aces and eight digs, and Addison finished the season 34 and 12.

Congratulations to every player, coach and family who made the trip. Check our homepage for more local scores and Friday night football updates.
