About Storm Shelters

Public Works opens three city storm shelters during a Tornado Watch. Each holds 90 people. They are behind the library at 202 Bangor Avenue SE, at C.W. Day Park at 203 Michelle Street NW and in Steppville at 1407 Commercial Street SE.
