Hanceville's Cipollari siblings will play a free Randy Travis Foundation benefit Nov. 7

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The six sibling bluegrass band from Hanceville performs Saturday, Nov. 7, from 6 to 8 p.m. at Terri Pines Country Club in Cullman. Tickets are free.

Hanceville's homegrown bluegrass band is taking the stage for a good cause. Cipollari, made up of six brothers and sisters, will perform Cipollari Live: A Fundraiser for the Randy Travis Foundation on Saturday, Nov. 7, from 6 to 8 p.m. at Terri Pines Country Club in Cullman.

Tickets are free, and you can reserve yours through the link in The Cullman Tribune's story. Savio plays mandolin, Thérèse and Rosalinda play fiddle, Cecilia plays guitar, Gianluca plays banjo and Giovanni plays dobro.

Longtime fans may remember the group as the Cotton Pickin' Kids. The siblings released their first album, Slemish Mountain, in 2020 and have built a big online following with performance videos and original songs.

The Randy Travis Foundation was started after the country star's 2013 stroke. It works toward a cure for viral cardiomyopathy and helps at risk children take part in arts and music programs. That makes a night of family bluegrass a perfect fit.
