About 500 seniors filled the fairgrounds for Senior Day at the Cullman County Fair

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The Cullman County Commission on Aging says roughly 500 people 55 and older came out Wednesday for free admission, a free lunch, live music and a moment honoring veterans.

Wednesday was a big day for Cullman County's seniors. About 500 people age 55 and older spent Oct. 21 at the Cullman County Fairgrounds for Senior Day at the Cullman County Fair, according to Commission on Aging Director Stephanie Lawson.

Admission, parking and lunch were all free. Lawson thanked the Lions Club for cooking extra corn dogs and hot dogs for the large crowd, and Jamie Looney and her Grand Ole Opry group provided the entertainment. Aveanna sponsored the event and Premier Bank sponsored the lunch. There were no rides for the group, but guests toured the exhibits and picked up candied apples.

One of the highlights was a tribute to veterans, who were called up and given certificates while a patriotic song played. Lawson said everyone was on their feet to thank them.

Several elected officials spoke as well. Lawson noted it was the final Fair Day stage appearance in their current roles for Sheriff Matt Gentry, Revenue Commissioner Barry Willingham and Commissioner Garry Marchman.

Looking for activities for seniors in Hanceville and around the county? The Commission on Aging posts its programs at co.cullman.al.us/coa and on its Facebook Page.
