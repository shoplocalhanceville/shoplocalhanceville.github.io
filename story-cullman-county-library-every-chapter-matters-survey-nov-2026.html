Cullman County Public Library adds a campaign manager for its $1 million Every Chapter Matters drive and wants your input

Published Nov 9, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The library's Every Chapter Matters campaign aims to raise $1 million to renovate and expand its 1967 building. New campaign manager Laney Hulsey says a community survey is open now, and help can mean volunteering or spreading the word, not just donating.

The Cullman County Public Library is working toward a bigger, better home, and it has added someone to help lead the way. The Cullman Tribune reports that Laney Hulsey has joined the library as capital campaign marketing and community engagement manager for Every Chapter Matters, the library's drive to raise $1 million for renovation and expansion.

The main library building in Cullman opened in 1967. Hulsey told the Tribune that the county has grown since then, and the current space can no longer fully fit the programs, technology, study areas and gathering spaces the library wants to offer. The plan calls for more room for educational programs, better technology and spaces for all ages.

The campaign has two goals: raising the money and making sure more people know what the library already offers. Hulsey will handle the campaign's social media, website and outreach, and she will work with local businesses, organizations, donors and volunteers.

Residents can weigh in right now. The library has a community survey open to learn which programs, services and resources matter most. Hulsey said support does not look the same for everyone: volunteering, attending fundraisers, partnering as a business or simply talking up the campaign all count.

The Cullman County Public Library system includes the Shirley Burden Public Library branch here in Hanceville at 201 Commercial St. Earlier this fall the library's Spooktacular 5K raised money for the same campaign.
