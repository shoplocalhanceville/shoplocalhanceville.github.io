Wallace State volleyball wins 14 straight and heads to the conference tournament at 30 and 5

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The Lions swept all five matches in the past week, including a home doubleheader with Southern Union on Thursday, and open ACCC tournament play in Montgomery on Oct. 31.

Wallace State volleyball did not drop a single set this past week. The Lions closed the regular season with five straight sweeps at Tom Drake Coliseum and now sit at 30 and 5 overall and 14 and 2 in the Alabama Community College Conference, according to the team's official results. That makes 14 wins in a row.

It started Saturday, Oct. 17, at the Wallace State Lions Invitational. The Lions beat Illinois Central 25 to 15, 26 to 24 and 25 to 17, then topped Reid State 25 to 9, 25 to 11 and 25 to 22.

On Wednesday they swept Calhoun 25 to 23, 25 to 12 and 25 to 18. The Cullman Tribune noted that the win made up for a four set loss at Calhoun back in September.

Thursday brought a conference doubleheader with Southern Union. Wallace State won the first match 25 to 23, 25 to 4 and 25 to 16 and the second 25 to 14, 25 to 12 and 25 to 10.

Two Lions also picked up weekly conference honors. The Tribune reported that sophomore Cheyenne Lucas was named ACCC Offensive Player of the Week and sophomore setter Darcy Alford was named ACCC Setter of the Week.

Next up is the ACCC Women's Volleyball Championship Tournament, set for Oct. 30 through Nov. 1 at the Multiplex at Cramton Bowl in Montgomery. The team schedule lists Wallace State's first match for Saturday, Oct. 31, at 3 p.m., with the opponent still to be set. Follow live scores in our Game Center.
