About Apply for SNAP Online (MyDHR)

Apply for food assistance online, check your case, renew and report changes. You will create a free account first, and your application goes to your county DHR office.

Who it serves: Alabama residents applying for or already getting SNAP.

How to get started: Cullman County still uses MyDHR as of October 2026. A few Alabama counties have moved to DHR's new ACES Self Service Portal, and MyDHR will tell you if yours has.
