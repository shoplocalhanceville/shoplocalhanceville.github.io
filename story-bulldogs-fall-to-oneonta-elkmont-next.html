Oneonta beats Hanceville 62 to 0; Bulldogs close the season at Elkmont next Friday

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The Bulldogs fell to 8 and 1 Oneonta in their final home game of the year Friday night at Ray Talley Stadium. Hanceville wraps up the regular season Oct. 30 at Elkmont.

Friday night at Ray Talley Stadium was a tough one for the Hanceville Bulldogs. Oneonta, one of the strongest teams in Class 3A, Region 6, won 62 to 0 in what AL.com called an early final in its statewide Week 10 coverage.

The loss drops Hanceville to 0 and 9 overall and 0 and 7 in region play. Oneonta came into the night at 8 and 1.

It was the last home game of the season, so thank you to the players, coaches, band, cheerleaders and every fan who came out to support the Bulldogs at home this fall.

The Bulldogs close the regular season on the road at Elkmont on Friday, Oct. 30, at 7 p.m. Follow the score in our Game Center.
