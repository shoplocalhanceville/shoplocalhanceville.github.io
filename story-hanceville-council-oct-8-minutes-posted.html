City Hall

Signed minutes from the Oct. 8 council meeting show new park addresses, a $30 Holly Market vendor fee and a pool leak

Published Friday, October 23, 2026 by Hanceville Today

The city posted the official minutes from the Oct. 8 Hanceville City Council meeting. They list new addresses planned for C.W. Day Park and the pool, a $30 fee for Holly Market vendors, and a pool leak crews still have not found.

The City of Hanceville has posted the official minutes from the Oct. 8 City Council meeting. Mayor Nolan Bradford and City Clerk Teja McIntyre signed them on Oct. 26. The biggest votes from that night, including $25,000 more for road paving and the vote to uphold the removal of Fire Chief Chris Hardin, were covered in our earlier story. The minutes add a few details residents may want to know.

C.W. Day Park is getting a new address. The minutes say the park and a funeral home currently share the same address, so the park will become 100 Park Rd. and the pool will become 200 Park Rd. The minutes do not give a date for the change, so keep using the current address for now.

Vendors in the Hanceville Holly Market will pay only a $30 fee, according to the council's discussion. The indoor holiday market is set for the Hanceville Civic Center on Saturdays in December.

The park director told the council the leak in the city pool still has not been found, and the pool keeps losing water and chemicals. Cooler weather should cut chemical use a lot. Asbestos has been removed from the Park House, security cameras have been ordered, and bills for the fence project have started to come in.

The Fire Department reported 116 calls for the month and 1,106 so far this year. Two firefighters are in firefighter survival training, and Assistant Fire Chief Absher was set to take a traffic incident management trainer class in Cullman on Oct. 13 and 14.

The council also named Mayor Bradford, Mayor Pro Tem Jana Shelton, Councilor Kim Brown and Josh Howell to the Policy and Procedures Committee working to bring back a city police department. Councilor Robert Powell suggested looking at Sprout as the city's internet provider. Votes on the credit card policy and several ordinances were pushed to later meetings, and the council took up most of them on Oct. 22.

The next regular council meeting is Thursday, Nov. 12, at City Hall, 112 Main Street SE. You can read the full minutes on the city's website.
