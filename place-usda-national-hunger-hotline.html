About USDA National Hunger Hotline

Talk with someone who can find meal sites, food banks and other food help near you. You can also text a question to 914-342-7744.

Who it serves: Anyone who needs food.

hunger hotline1-866-3-hungryfoodfederal

Hours

Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Central time.

Hours can change around holidays, so a quick call before you drive over is always a good idea.
