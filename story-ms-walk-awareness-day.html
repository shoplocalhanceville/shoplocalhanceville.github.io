Cullman marks MS Awareness Day with a free walk at Heritage Park today

Published Saturday, October 17, 2026 by Hanceville Today

Mayor Woody Jacobs proclaimed Oct. 17 Multiple Sclerosis Awareness Day. The Move Over MS Walk runs 8 a.m. to noon at Heritage Park with music, free snacks and a kids costume contest.

Saturday is Multiple Sclerosis Awareness Day in Cullman. Mayor Woody Jacobs signed the proclamation earlier this month so neighbors living with MS, and the people who love them, know the city is in their corner.

The Move Over MS Walk is today, Oct. 17, from 8 a.m. to noon on the Olive Street side of Heritage Park. North Central Neurology Associates, the Joanne P. LaGanke MS Center and MS Views and News are hosting. Registration is free online.

Organizers promise live music from The Overtones, free biscuits, fruit, coffee and doughnuts, plus giveaways from pharmaceutical partners. Kids can enter a Halloween costume contest, and a raffle supports the event. Raffle tickets are $5 each or five for $20.

Dr. Christopher LaGanke founded the Joanne P. LaGanke MS Center in memory of his mother. The center focuses on patient care, research and clinical trials. If you can make it, pack a jacket for the cool morning and come cheer folks on.
