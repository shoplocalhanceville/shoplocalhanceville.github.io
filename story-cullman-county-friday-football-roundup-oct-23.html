Friday night roundup: Addison wins region title, Vinemont clinches seventh straight playoff trip

Published Friday, October 24, 2026 by Hanceville Today

Addison finished a perfect 10 and 0 regular season with a Class 1A, Region 7 title, Vinemont rallied late to make the playoffs again, and Cold Springs, Cullman and Good Hope all won big on the last region night of the season.

It was a big Friday night across Cullman County as region play wrapped up and playoff spots were settled.

Addison beat previously unbeaten Hackleburg 33 to 7 at home, scoring 33 straight points and picking off four passes. The Bulldogs finished the regular season 10 and 0, went 7 and 0 in Class 1A, Region 7, and earned the region's No. 1 playoff seed.

Vinemont won 12 to 7 over Susan Moore on senior night. Josh Rhodes hit Travis Speer for a 19 yard touchdown on fourth down with 4:17 left, sending the Eagles to their seventh playoff appearance in a row. Vinemont finished the regular season 6 and 4, and 4 and 3 in Class 3A, Region 6.

Cold Springs won 56 to 6 at Carbon Hill to improve to 8 and 1, its third straight win. Cullman pulled away for a 42 to 13 win at Jackson Olin in Class 5A, Region 5 play, and Good Hope scored 42 straight points to beat Hayden 42 to 7 on senior night, moving to 7 and 2 overall and 6 and 1 in Class 4A, Region 8.

It was a tougher night for a few county teams. Etowah beat Holly Pond 43 to 12, dropping the Broncos to 2 and 7. Deshler beat West Point 55 to 14 in the Warriors' season finale, and Oneonta shut out Hanceville 62 to 0 at Ray Talley Stadium.

Hanceville closes its season Friday, Oct. 30, at Elkmont. Follow that game in our Game Center.
